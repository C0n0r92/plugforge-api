In addition to the Admissions Notice, the following is also provided for your information

               Criteria for enrolment in autism class in Mary, Mother of Hope JNS
       Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for
     enrolment to an autism class, incorporating the Dept. of Education and NCSE. policies &
               circulars, Circular_Letter_0039-2025_EN_final.pdf are as follows:

        1. Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-
           disciplinary report)
        2. A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of need/s
           evidenced in the professional reports
        3. Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
           recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate to
           best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same.
        4. A letter of eligibility from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and that the
           child has the required diagnosis and recommendation for a special class for autism is
           required for special class or special school placement.

In the event that the number of children seeking enrolment in our special class for children with autism
exceeds the number of places available, the following criteria in the order listed below, will be used to
prioritise children who have met the eligibility criteria for enrolment in a special class for children with
autism as outlined in section 4 of this Admissions Policy:
Category 1 - Children already enrolled in the school
Category 2 - All children living within the catchment area And Brothers & Sisters (including stepsiblings
& foster children resident at same address) of children currently enrolled in Mary, Mother of Hope
Schools
Category 3 - Children of current staff members of Mary, Mother of Hope schools (including stepchildren
& foster       children resident at same address)
Category 4 - All children from outside the catchment area
In the event of the number of children in category (1) exceeding the number of places available in a
given year, the Board of Management will offer places based on chronological age of the children with
the youngest applicants given a place first.
Where two children are tied for one place, the place will be allocated by lottery which will be overseen
by three members of the Board of Management
The same criteria will be adopted in relation to pool (2) and then (3) and then (4) until all places are
filled. This process will be repeated each time a place becomes available in the ASD class
All unsuccessful applicants will be placed on a waiting list, in accordance with the above criteria for
places that may become available during the same school year.
