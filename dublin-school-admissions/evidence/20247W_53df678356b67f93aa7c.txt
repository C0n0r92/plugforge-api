Junior infants, Autism Class and Other Classes Applications and Decision dates for Admission:

Junior Infants

All applications to Junior Infants in Scoil Ghrainne CNS will only be accepted after 1st October 2026 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 22nd October 2026. Any applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

All applicants to the Junior Infant Classes will be notified of an offer of a place or refusal of admission by 12th November 2026.

Successful applicants to the Junior Infants Group must confirm their acceptance of an offer of admission by completing and returning the Acceptance Form on or before 26th November, 2026.
Autism Classes

All Applications for our Autism Class will only be accepted after the 22nd October 2026 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 12th November 2026.
Any applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.


All applicants to the Autism Classes will be notified of an offer of a place or refusal of admission by 3rd December 2026.

Successful applicants to the Junior Infants Group must confirm their acceptance of an offer of admission by completing and returning the Acceptance Form on or before 17th December 2026.

Applications to other classes

All applications for admissions to other classes (Senior Infants to 6th Class) will be accepted after 22nd October 2026 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 12th November 2026. Any applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

All applicants to these classes will be notified of an offer of a place or refusal of admission by 3rd December 2026.

Successful applicants to these classes must confirm their acceptance of an offer of admission by completing and returning the Acceptance Form on or before 17th December 2026.
IMPORTANT ACCEPTANCE NOTEFailure to accept an offer of admission by the relevant deadline may result in the offer being withdrawn. For late or subsequent-round offers, the Acceptance Form must be returned by the date stated in the letter offering the place.
Please be reminded that, in line with the school’s admission Policy and the Education (Admission to School) Act 2018, all waiting lists going forward are only in place for the academic year to which they apply. Therefore, if a child is seeking a place in the Autism Class and is not successful in a given academic year or is on a waiting list for this year, a new application must be made for the following year.
