Annual Admission Notice 2027/2028 PART 1 – Admissions to the 2027/2028 school year The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants 2027/2028: The school will commence accepting applications for admission on October 1st 2026. The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 6th November 2026. The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 7th December 2026. The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 3 weeks. Special Class Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2026/2027 The following are the dates applicable for admission to the school’s Special Class which caters for children with Autism: The school will commence accepting applications for admission to the special class on October 1st 2026. The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to the special class on 6th November 2026. All applications for the special class will require a eligibility letter from the NCSE to process the application. To apply for this the Parents Notify form needs to be completed before the 1st of October. This form can be found at https://ncse.ie/notify-ncse-special-class-special-school The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application for admission to the special class is 7th December 2026. The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 3 weeks. NOTE: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy. Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn. Please note: Pre-School service attendance does not guarantee admission to St Audoen’s NS, this is a provision of The Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018. Please see St Audoen’s Admission Policy for further info. Number of places being made available in Junior Infants 2025/2026 The number of places being made available in Junior Infants is 22. The number of places being made available in the classes for children with Autism is 1 (unless a child moves school or transitions to Mainstream).
