All applications to the Junior Infants in Scoil Ghráinne CNS will only be accepted after the 1st October 2024 and the closing date for receipt of applications is 31st January 2025. Applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

All applications to the ASD class in Scoil Ghráinne CNS will only be accepted after the 1st November 2024 and the closing date for receipt of applications is March 1st 2025.

All transfer applications to the, Senior Infants, First Class, Second Class, Third Class, Fourth Class, Fifth Class or Sixth Class in Scoil Ghráinne CNS will only be accepted after 1st October 2024 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 31st May 2025. Applications receives after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.


Junior infants and Autism Class Applications and Decision dates for Admission:
All applications to Junior Infants in Scoil Ghrainne CNS will only be accepted after 01/10/2024 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 31/01/2025. Any applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy

All Applications for our Autism Class will only be accepted after the 01/11/2024 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 1st March 2025.

All transfer applications to Senior Infants, First Class, Second Class, Third Class, Fourth Class, Fifth Class or Sixth Class in Scoil Ghrainne CNS other than to the special Class, will only be accepted after the 01/10/2024 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 31st May 2025. Applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

Please be reminded that, in line with the school’s admission Policy and the Education (Admission to School) Act 2018, all waiting lists going forward are only in place for the academic year to which they apply. Therefore, if a child is seeking a place in the Special Class and is not successful in a given academic year or is on a waiting list for this year, a new application must be made for the following year.
