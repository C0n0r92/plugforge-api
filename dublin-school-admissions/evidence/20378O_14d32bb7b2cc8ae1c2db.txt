Applications must be submitted by post in hard copy to the address above and must
include:

   1. A completed Application Form for Admission
   2. a copy of the original documented and confirmed diagnosis of ASD.
   3. a recent written recommendation from a certified psychologist or an assessment

   4. Special School, Eligibility letter from the NCSE
Note: In accordance with the school’s Admission Policy, the school will not consider late
applications. Nor will the school accept incomplete applications that do not include the
documents outlined above.
Data Protection: As per our data protection policy, all applications will be retained for the
