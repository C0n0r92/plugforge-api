 PART 1 - Admissions to the 2026 school year

 Application and Decision Dates for admission to Junior Infants 2026
  Criteria for Enrolment to Junior Infants

  Applicants must be at least four years of age before the First of June of the year in which they apply.
  Compulsory attendance at school does not apply until the age of six years. Junior Infants must start school
  during the month of September, as places will not be reserved after the 30th September.

  The catchment area, for enrolment purposes consists of the following housing estates in the parish of
  Huntstown: Castaheany & Hansfield estates (off Littlepace Rd.), Deerhaven, Hazelbury, Hunters Run,
  Littlepace, Pheasant Run.

  The school will commence accepting applications for admission on                           01/10/2025
  The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on                             31/10/2025

  The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is      21/11/2025

  The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is  28/11/2025
 Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn

 Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the
 school’s admission policy.
