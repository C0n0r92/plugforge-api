Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2027-28
The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants


Tosóidh an scoil ag glacadh le hiarratais i gcomhair ligean isteach ar

The school will commence accepting applications for admission on
01/10/2026


Stopfaidh an scoil ag glacadh le hiarratais i gcomhair ligean isteach ar
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on

29/01/2027
Is é an dáta faoina gcuirfear an cinneadh maidir lena n-iarratas in iúl d’iarratasóirí ná

The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is


12/02 /2027


Is í an tréimhse nach mór d’iarratasóirí a dheimhniú go bhfuil siad ag glacadh le tairiscint
ar ligean isteach laistigh di ná 7 lá ón lá a bhfuarthas an cinneadh*


The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is
7 days from receipt of offer*



*Mura nglactar le tairiscint taobh istigh den am atá leagtha síos thuas, d'fhéadfaí an
 tairiscint a tharraingt siar

*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer
 being withdrawn



Nóta: Measfaidh an scoil iarratais dhéanacha agus eiseoidh cinntí orthu de réir polasaí
iontrála na scoile.

Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with
the school’s admission policy.
