 All applications to the Junior Infants or the Special Class (CSE) in Lucan CNS will only be accepted after the
 23/10/24 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 15/11/24. Applications received after this date
 will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.


 All transfer applications to Senior Infants, First Class, Second Class, Third Class, Fourth Class, Fifth Class or Sixth
 Class in Lucan CNS (for 2025/2026) other than to the Special Class, will only be accepted after the 23/10/24 and
 the closing date for receipt of applications is the 13/06/2025. Applications received after this date will be
considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy. Please note that
as with all applications, our current classes Junior Infants to Fifth Class are currently full and therefore an
application does not mean a place will be available to offer for the next academic year (2025/26).


Please be reminded that, in line with the school’s Admission Policy and the Education (Admission to Schools)
Act 2018, all waiting lists going forward are only in place for the academic year to which they apply. Therefore,
if a child is seeking a place in the Special Class and is not successful in a given academic year or is on a waiting
list for this year, a new application must be made for the following year.
