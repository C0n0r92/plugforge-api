PART 3 – Application Requirements
All applications for a place in the school shall be submitted to the School Principal on the approved
Application Form – available on www.kogrange.com

Only those Application Forms that have been properly completed, signed, dated & accompanied
by the relevant supporting documentation will be considered.
For an application to be considered complete, all required accompanying documentation must be
provided before the closing date.

Applications for Admission to Junior Infants

An Application Form must be accompanied by

               i.       A birth certificate for the student in respect to whom the application has been
                        made (the “Applicant Student”).


               ii.      Proof of address, at the time of application, in the form of an original gas,
                        electricity, or fixed landline bill in the Applicant’s name or in one of the
                        Applicant’s names where there is more than one applicant which must be
                        dated no later than three months before the closing date.


               iii.     A statement confirming that the Applicant Student is a member of a minority
                        religion, where applicable.


               iv.      Evidence to support the statement that the Applicant Student is a member of
                        a minority religion, where applicable.

       i.           A birth certificate for the student in respect to whom the application has been

Applications for Admission to the NEST – Autism Class
An Application Form must be accompanied by

              i.       A birth certificate for the student in respect to whom the application has been
                       made (the “Applicant Student”).


              ii.      Proof of address, at the time of application, in the form of an original gas,
                       electricity, or fixed landline bill in the Applicant’s name or in one of the
                       Applicant’s names where there is more than one applicant which must be
                       dated no later than three months before the closing date.


              iii.     The following documentation as specified by the DES and NCSE


Children and young people are eligible for enrolment in an Autism special class when the
following is provided in support of such an application:

     Professional report(s) outlining:
    Diagnosis of special educational needs (e.g. Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11
     (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-disciplinary report)
AND
    A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of need/s
     evidenced in the professional reports
AND
    Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
     recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate to
     best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same
AND
    A letter from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and that the child has
     the required diagnosis and recommendation for a special class for Autism
