PART 1 - Admissions to the 2027/2028 school year

Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2027/2028 school year
The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants

The school will commence accepting applications for admission on  
 October 1st  2026@10am
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on  
October 22nd 2026
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is     
November 12th 2026
The date by which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is
November 26th 2026

Note: applications made after October 22nd 2026 will be processed as late applications. The school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy.

Failure of an applicant to accept an offer by November 26th 2026 may result in the offer being withdrawn.

Other Year Groups Application and Decision Dates for admission to the 2027/2028 school year.
The following are the dates applicable for admission to other year groups (Senior Infants to 6th Class and including Autism Class).

The school will commence accepting applications for admission to other year groups on
 10th January 2027@10am
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to other year groups on  
22nd  January 2027 
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is     
29th January 2027
The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is
12th February 2027

Note: applications made after January 22nd 2027 will be processed as late applications. The school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy.

Failure of an applicant to accept an offer by February 12th 2027 may result in the offer being withdrawn.
