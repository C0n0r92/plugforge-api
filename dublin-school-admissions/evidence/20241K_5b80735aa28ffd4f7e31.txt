                    Criteria for enrolment in autism class in Scoil Choilm CNS:

      Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for
    enrolment to an autism class, incorporating the Dept. of Education and NCSE. policies &
               circulars, Circular_Letter_0039-2025_EN_final.pdf are as follows:

     1. Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-
        disciplinary report)
     2. A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of need/s
        evidenced in the professional reports
     3. Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
        recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate to
        best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same.
     4. A letter of eligibility from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and that the
        child has the required diagnosis and recommendation for a special class for autism is
        required for special class or special school placement.


 Oversubscription priority criterion applied in Scoil Choilm CNS
 To be applied in the event of there being more applicants for an available special class place. Listed in
 order of priority considerations. Please see our Admissions Policy on our school website.

 1. The child is 4 years of age or older, on or before the 31st of May, in the year of entry. The children in our
    special class will commence their school in Junior classes. If two or more Students are tied on this
    criterion then the next selection criteria in the list will be applied to those students to determine the
    order of priority of those students.
 2. The child is currently enrolled in our mainstream setting with a diagnosis of ASD and has a
    recommendation for a special class.
 3. The child has siblings currently attending Scoil Choilm CNS (including stepsiblings, residing at the same
    address if they meet the criteria in relation to age)
 4. The child resides in the catchment area. The catchment area, for enrolment purposes is in the Dublin 15
    area and is outlined in Appendix 1 of our Admissions Policy
 5. The child resides outside the catchment area unless the area in which they live is within the catchment area
    of another Community National School which has vacancies in their special class.

       In the event that there are two or more students tied for a place or places in the selection criteria
       categories 2-5 (the number of applicants exceeds the number of remaining places), places will be
       offered or added to a waiting list by the later date of birth of the student.
