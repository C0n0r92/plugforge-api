Decisions of the Board of Management on admission are based on the school’s admission policy which
includes the following provisions: -

    •   Children within the correct age profile for our Special Classes in a mainstream setting, with
        appropriate diagnostic material/reports and a letter of eligibility issued by our local SENO are offered
        places on a first come/first served basis, based on the date and time of receipt of the fully completed
        application form and all supporting documentation.

    •   Receipt of each application for admission with all necessary reports after the commencement of the
        Application period and prior to last Monday in April each year is acknowledged and filed according to
        date of receipt.
Note: The school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period may result in the offer being withdrawn.
