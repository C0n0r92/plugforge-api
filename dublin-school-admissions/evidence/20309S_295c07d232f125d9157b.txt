Application and Decision Dates for admission to Junior Infants 2025

 Criteria for Enrolment to Junior Infants
 Applicants must be at least four years of age before the First of June of the year in which they
 apply. Compulsory attendance at school does not apply until the age of six years. Junior Infants must
 start school during the month of September, as places will not be reserved after the 30th September.

 The catchment area, for enrolment purposes consists of the following housing estates in the parish
 of Huntstown: Castaheany & Hansfield estates (off Littlepace Rd.), Deerhaven, Hazelbury, Hunters
 Run, Littlepace, Pheasant Run.
