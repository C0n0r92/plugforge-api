PART 1 - Admissions to St Paul’s Special School for the 2027/2028 school year

Application and Decision Dates for admission to St Paul’s Special School

The following are the dates applicable for admission to Main School

The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 1st October 2026
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission @ 3:00pm on 21st December 2026
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 29th
January 2027

Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with
the school’s admission policy.
