





UPDATE 14/07/2025

Belmayne CSS will establish an additional class to that of Group 1. This is an additional third-class Group 1C (Aged 5 years to 9 years in line with criteria set out for Group 1 for the 2025/2026 academic year. Places therein will be offered to those who are next on the waiting list for that Group 1C, which was compiled in accordance with the admission policy.



UPDATE 07/07/2025

Where Belmayne CSS received no applications for Group 4 for the 2025/2026 academic year during the application window for same, it has been decided to use the school capacity and resources that would have been allocated to that Group 4 to establish a second Group 1B (Aged 5 years to 9 years in line with Criteria set out for Group 1) for the 2025/2026 academic year instead. Places therein will be offered to those who are next on the waiting list for that Group 1B, which was compiled in accordance with the admission policy.
