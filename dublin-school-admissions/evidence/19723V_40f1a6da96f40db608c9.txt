Admissions Queen of Angels PS Annual Admission Notice 2027/2028 School Year Admission Policy and Application Form A copy of the school's Admission Policy and the Application Form for Admission for the 2027/2028 school year is available as follows: Visit www.qoa.ie Application forms are available via the link on the front page. Part 1 – Admissions to the 2027/2028 School Year Application and Decision Dates The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants. 1. The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 28th September 2026 2. The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 16th October 2026 3. The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 13th November 2026 4. The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 14 days Important: Applications made after the 16th of October will be processed as late applications. The school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school's admission policy. Important: Failure of an applicant to accept an offer within fourteen days may result in the offer being withdrawn. Special Class Application and Decision Dates The following are the dates applicable for admission to the school's Special Classes which cater for autistic children. Note: The figures set out below in relation to the Special Classes are subject to change dependent on whether current students in the Special Classes retain their place in the school and whether a student(s) transfers into the school after the publication of this Notice. 1. The school will commence accepting applications for admission to the special class on 1st October 2026 2. The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to the special class on 29th April 2027 3. The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application for admission to the special class is 14th May 2027 4. The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 7 days after the offer is made Important: Applications made after the 14th of May will be processed as late applications. The school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school's admission policy. Important: Failure of an applicant to accept an offer within seven days may result in the offer being withdrawn. Other Year Groups Applications for admission into classes Senior Infants to 6th class will be considered on an individual basis. Important: Failure of an applicant to accept an offer within seven days may result in the offer being withdrawn.
