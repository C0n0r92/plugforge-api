All applications to the Junior Infants or the Special Classes (for ALL class groups) in Scoil Chormaic CNS will only
be accepted after the 02/12/2024 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 16/12/2024. Applications
received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s
Admission Policy.
All transfer applications to Senior Infants, First Class, Second Class, Third Class, Fourth Class, Fifth Class or Sixth
Class in Scoil Chormaic CNS other than to the Special Classes, will only be accepted after the 16/12/2024 and the
closing date for receipt of applications is the 10/01/2025.
Applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with
the school’s Admission Policy.
Please be reminded that, in line with the school’s Admission Policy and the Education (Admission to Schools)
Act 2018, all waiting lists going forward are only in place for the academic year to which they apply. Therefore,
if a child is seeking a place in the Special Class and is not successful in a given academic year or is on a waiting
list for this year, a new application must be made for the following year
