Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2027-2028 school year The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants. The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 1 st October 2026. The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 1 st November 2026. The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 23rd November 2026, or within three weeks of the date of receipt application in the case of late applications. The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is two weeks* i.e. 7 th December 2026. *Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy.
