An Cosán Community Special School will cooperate with the National Council for Special Education in the
performance by the Council of its functions under the Education for Persons with Special Educational Needs Act
2004 relating to the provision of education to children with special educational needs, including in particular by
the provision and operation of a special class or classes when requested to do so by the Council.

An Cosán Community Special School will comply with any direction served on the patron or the board of
management, as the case may be, under section 37A and any direction served on the board of management under
section 67(4B) of the Education Act.
