                        Criteria for enrolment in autism class in our school:

      Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for
   enrolment to an autism class, incorporating the Dept. of Education and NCSE. policies &
   circulars, https://www.gov.ie/en/circular/f16ca-new-measures-to-support-the-forward-
                    planning-for-special-education-provision/ are as follows:

     1. Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-
        disciplinary report)
     2. A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of need/s
        evidenced in the professional reports
     3. Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
        recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate to
        best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same.




 Oversubscription priority criterion applied in Powerstown ETNS
 To be applied in the event of there being more applicants for an available special class place. Listed in
 order of priority considerations
    1. Must be 4 years of age by 1st of June on year of enrolment
     2. Current students of the school/students who have been offered a place in the mainstream school
        for the coming September who meet age criteria as detailed in Admissions Notice (to correspond
        with current enrolees)
     3. Applicant students who are siblings of children, enrolled in the school at the same time who meet
        age criteria as detailed in Admissions Notice (to correspond with current enrolees)
     4. Children living within the Catchment Area of Tyrrlestown, Hollystown and Mulhuddart who
        meet age criteria as detailed in Admissions Notice
     5. Children living within D15 who meet age criteria as detailed in Admission’s Notice
     6. Children living outside the Catchment Area who meet age criteria as detailed in Admission’s Notice
          (to correspond with current enrolees)
     7. Revisit categories 1 – 5, prioritising children closest in age to the age criteria detailed in Admission’s
          Notice
    In the event that there are two or more students tied for a place or places in any of the selection
    criteria criterion above (the number of applicants exceeds the number of remaining places), places
    will be offered or added to a waiting list by way of a lottery of completed applications.
