Procedures for admission of students to other years & during the school year

New Applications Senior Infants to 6th class *Immediate Start*

Applications for these classes will be considered, subject to places being available in the relevant class(es) and having regard to existing waiting lists. 
Applications for these classes must be made available in writing via application form.  
Child must be transferring from another primary school or another appropriate educational setting.
Parents must furnish PETNS with reports from previous school prior to enrolment. 
These applications will be considered on the basis of criteria set out in Admission’s Notice
Please note that your child’s previous school will be contacted by Powerstown ETNS prior to enrolment / your child starting school. 
Your child must transfer to the same class level as they are presently in (i.e. children are not permitted to “skip” a class”).
Your child’s age must correspond with our age criteria whereby he/she is 4 years of age by 1st of June before commencing school
Please refer to Appendix 2 which details class and grade levels across countries.
Once offered a place, child must start school within 7 working days.
If child does not start school within 7 working days, the offer of place is withdrawn.

New Applications Senior Infants to 6th class *forthcoming academic year*

Parents/Guardians are encouraged to complete expression of interest in enrolment form. 
Application forms are accepted from the first (school) week of May (4/5/22)
Parents/Guardians have 7 working days to return application forms.
Applications for these classes will be considered, subject to places being available in the relevant class(es) and having regard to existing waiting lists. 
Places are offered by the 30/05/22.
Places must be accepted by 07/06/22. If place is not accepted by this date, the offer is withdrawn and place offered to next child on our waiting list. 
Child must start school within the first 7 days of the forthcoming school year*.  If child does not start school within the first 7 days, the offer of a place is withdrawn and place is offered to next child on our waiting list.
*The Board of Management may consider exceptional circumstances, if brought to their attention by the parents/guardians. 
Child must be transferring from another primary school or another appropriate educational setting.
Parents must furnish PETNS with reports from previous school prior to enrolment. 
These applications will be considered on the basis of the sibling criteria.
Please note that your child’s previous school will be contacted by Powerstown ETNS prior to enrolment / your child starting school. 
Your child must transfer to the same class level as they are presently in (i.e. children are not permitted to “skip” a class”).
Your child’s age must correspond with our age criteria whereby he/she is 4 years of age by 1st of June before commencing school.
Please refer to Appendix 2 which details class and grade levels across countries.

Oversubscription: Senior Infants – 6th class

If Powerstown ETNS is oversubscribed, the school will, when deciding on applications for admission, apply the following selection criteria in the order listed below. It will be applied to those applications that are received within the timeline for receipt of applications as set out in the school’s annual admission notice for other year groups. Students must be moving to the next year group if the application is for September or to the same year if applying to move during the school year: 

Priority Category 1:
Applicant students who are siblings of children, enrolled in the school and applicants age corresponds with being 4 years of age on or before 1st June of the year of admission into Junior Infants. 

Priority Category 2:
Applicants age corresponds with having reached 4 years of age on or before 1st June of the year of admission into Junior Infants.

Priority Category 3:
In the event that there are two or more students tied for a place or places in any of the selection criteria categories above (the number of applicants exceeds the number of remaining places), places will be offered or added to a waiting list by the earlier date of birth of the student. 





Mainstream Class size

In 2018 the Department of Education reduced the pupil teacher ratio to 26:1 (average). Powerstown Educate Together National School endeavours to use the pupil teacher ratio as decided by the Department of Education as a guide. 
In determining admissions, the Chairperson shall take account of Department of Education and Skills Regulations in relation to staffing schedule, class size and class average.  The Chairperson shall also have regard to issues such as physical space, multi-grade classes, and the presence of children with additional needs.

Annually the Board of Management will discuss staffing schedule, class sizes and needs and decide on pupil teacher ratio for the academic year and minutes of same recorded.
