                     Critéir don Ionrollú sa Rang Uathachais (Rang na Réaltaí) i SCOIL OILIBHÉIR
Faoi réir áiteanna leordhóthaineacha inár Rang Uathachais a bheith curtha ar fáil, seo a leanas na critéir don ionrollú, a
chuimsíonn polasaithe agus ciorcláin na Roinne Oideachais agus na CNOS, Circular_Letter_0039-2025_EN_final.pdf are
as follows:
    1. Diagnóis Uathachais: DSM IV/V nó ICD 10/11 (shíceolaí, síciatraí, tuairisc ildisciplíneach)

    2. Léiriú tuisceana ar chastacht leibhéil iomlán riachtana(i)s an pháiste le feiceáil sna tuairiscí ghairmiúla

    3. Agus déine nó castacht riachtanais thacaíochta an pháiste curtha san áireamh, moladh soiléir gairmiúil maidir
       le cén suíomh oideschais is oiriúnaí le freastal ar riachtanais an pháiste, mar aon le réasúnaíocht don mhladh
       sin.

    4. Litir cháilitheachta ón CNOS le deimhniú go bhfuil aithne acu ar an an bpáiste agus go bhfuil an diagnóis agus
       an moladh riachtanach acu don Rang Speisialta don Uachtachas nó don socrúchán i scoil speisialta.


                                               Critéir Iontrála I SCOIL OILIBHÉIR
Is Gaelscoil í Scoil Oilibhéir agus ní dhéanann sí idirdhealú nuair a chuireann sí leibhéal líofachta an pháiste sa Ghaeilge
san áireamh mar chritéir Ligean Isteach sa scoil seo. Luíonn sé seo le Polasaí Iontrála na ranganna príomhshrutha.

                                Critéir Thosaíochta I gCás an Ró-Éilimh I SCOIL OILIBHÉIR

 Le cur i bhfeidhm sa chás go bhfuil níos mó iarrthóirí ná áiteanna ar fái sa Rang Speisialta. Liostaithe in ord
tosaíochta

1. Is gá don pháiste a bheith 4 bliana d’aois roimh 1 Aibreán na bliana ionrollaithe
2. Daltaí reatha na scoile
3. Deartháireacha/Deirfiúracha* le dalta reatha na scoile
4. Páiste na foirne
5. Páistí gur deimhin leis an scoil gur ghnóthaigh an dalta leibhéal líofachta sa Ghaeilge agus gur dócha go rachadh an
   líofacht sin ar gcúl mura ligfí isteach i scoil lán-Ghaeilge é/í.**
6. Páistí a bhfuil cumas Gaeilge acu ar leibhéal a ligfeadh don pháiste feidhmiú agus foghlaim sa rang agus nach mbeadh
   ar dhaltaí eile iompú ar an mBéarla chun cumarsáid a dhéanamh. Beidh ar an tuismitheoir/caomhnóir fianaise den
   chaighdeán seo a chur ar fáil. Is ag an mbord bainistíochta a bheidh an cinneadh faoin gcumas seo feidhmiú trí mheán
   na Gaeilge sna cásanna seo
* Deartháireacha, deirfiúracha, leath-shiblíní, páistí altramaigh nó uchtaithe.
**Beidh an dualgas iomlán ar thuismitheoirí/chaomhnóirí fianaise leordhóthanach a chur ar fáil chun leibhéal líofachta sa
Ghaeilge a léiriú don bhord. Féach eolas breise sa cháipéis ábhartha atá ar fáil ón scoil.

Sa chás go bhfuil beirt iarrthóirí nó níos mó ceangailte le haghaidh áite nó áiteanna in aon cheann de na catagóirí critéir
roghnúcháin thuas (agus Iíon na n-iarratasóirí níos mó ná líon na n-áiteanna atá fágtha), tabharfar áit don dalta is sine
eatarthu.

                       Criteria for enrolment in autism class in SCOIL OILIBHÉIR:

  Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for enrolment to an
           autism class, incorporating the Dept. of Education and NCSE. policies & circulars,
                         Circular_Letter_0039-2025_EN_final.pdf are as follows:

      1.   Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-disciplinary report)

      2.   A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of need/s evidenced
           in the professional reports

      3.   Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional recommendation
           as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate to best meet the child’s needs,
           along with the rationale for same.

      4.   A letter of eligibility from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and that the child
           has the required diagnosis and recommendation for a special class for autism is required for special
           class or special school placement.

                          Oversubscription priority criterion applied in SCOIL OILIBHÉIR

 To be applied in the event of there being more applicants for an available special class place. Listed in order
 of priority considerations

     1.   Current pupils of the school
     2.   Siblings* of existing pupils of the school.
     3.   Children of Staff.
     4.   Children who have attained a determined level of fluency in Irish which would be likely to decline if
          he/she is not admitted to an all-Irish school **
      5. Children whose Irish language ability is at a level that would allow the child to function and learn in class
          and where other pupils would not have to turn to English to communicate. The parent/guardian will
          be required to provide evidence of this standard. The board of management will have the final decision
          in these cases.
      6. In the case of two or more applicants being tied for a place or places in any of the above categories of
          selection criteria (and the amount of applicants exceeding the amount of places remaining), the place will
          be given to the eldest of them.
  * Siblings, half-siblings, foster children, adopted children,
  **The onus will be on parents/guardians to provide sufficient evidence to demonstrate to the board the
 required level of fluency in Irish. See further information in the relevant document available from the school.
