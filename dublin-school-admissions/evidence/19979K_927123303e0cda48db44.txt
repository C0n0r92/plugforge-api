PART 1 - Admissions to the 2022/2023 school year

Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2022/2023
The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants


 The school will commence accepting applications for admission on                                 10th January 2023
 (This date cannot be before October 1st of the year preceding the start of the school year for
 which admission is being sought)
 The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on                                   16th March 2023
 (There must be a minimum period of 3 weeks between this date and the commencement date
 for accepting applications for admission)
 The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is            8thApril 2023
 (This date must be within 3 weeks of the closing date for receipt of application or within 3
 weeks of the application date for late applications)
 The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is           25th April 2023 to
                                                                                                  13th May 2023


*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn



Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission
policy.
This school shall admit each student seeking admission except where –

i.       the school is oversubscribed (please see Admissions Policy for further details)

ii.      a parent of a student, when required by the principal in accordance with section 23(4) of the Education
(Welfare) Act 2000, fails to confirm in writing that the code of behaviour of the school is acceptable to him or her
and that he or she shall make all reasonable efforts to ensure compliance with such code by the student

iii.     Where a student is less than 4 years of age on the 1 September of the school year concerned as according to
Rule 64 for National Schools.

iv.      St. Kevin’s NS is a Roman Catholic school and may refuse to admit as a student a person who is not of
Roman Catholic belief where it is proved that the refusal is essential to maintain the ethos of the school.
