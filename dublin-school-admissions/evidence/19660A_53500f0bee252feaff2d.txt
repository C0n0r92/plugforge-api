             Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2022/2023
 The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants

 The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 6th         6th January 2022
 January 2022.
 The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 26th         26th January 2022
 January 2022. All applications must be submitted, by hand, to the
 school office.

 In order for an application to be considered complete, the parents of the
 applicant must provide;

  An application form, completed in full.

  The applicant’s original birth certificate. A photocopy will be made in
   the office and the original document returned.

  If seeking to avail of priority due to residency, two items of
   documentation will be required to prove residency in the parish of
   Rush. Each document must be in the name of one of the applicant’s
   parent(s), which must be dated no later than three months prior to
   the date of the application being made. Only original documents will
   be accepted. A photocopy will be made in the office and the original
   document returned.

  Applications which are incomplete, not signed, not dated and/or do
    not include the required documentation, will not be processed.
