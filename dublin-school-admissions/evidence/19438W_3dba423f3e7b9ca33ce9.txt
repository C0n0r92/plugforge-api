PART 1 - Admissions to the 2027/28 school year. 
Application and Decision Dates for Admission to 2027/28
The following are the dates applicable for admission to 3rd Class:
The school will commence accepting applications for admission on
3rd November 2026
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on
21st November 2026
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is
3rd December 2026
The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is*
17th December 2026
*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn.
Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

Special Class Application and Decision Dates for admission to the 2027/28 school year
The following are the dates applicable for admission to the school’s special class, which caters for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder:
The school will commence accepting applications for admission to the special class on
3rd November 2026
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to the special class on
21st November 2026
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application for admission to the special class is
10th December 2026
The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission to the special class is*
18th December 2026
*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn.
