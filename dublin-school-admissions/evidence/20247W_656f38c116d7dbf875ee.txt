  Annual Admission Notice 2026/2027
The following Annual Admission Notice has been prepared by the board of Scoil Ghráinne CNS in respect of the admission of students to the school for the school year 2026/2027. This notice was published on 01/10/2025.
A copy of the school’s Admission Policy and the Application Form for Admission for the 2026/2027 is available as follows: To download at:  https://www.scoilghrainnecns.ie/enrol/

On request: By emailing [school email omitted] or upon request from the school office.

Junior infants, Autism Class and Other Classes Applications and Decision dates for Admission:

Junior Infants

All applications to Junior Infants in Scoil Ghrainne CNS will only be accepted after 1st October 2025 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 22nd October 2025. Any applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

All applicants to the Junior Infant Classes will be notified of an offer of a place or refusal of admission by 12th November 2025.

Successful applicants to the Junior Infants Group must confirm their acceptance of an offer of admission by completing and returning the Acceptance Form on or before 26th November, 2025.






Autism Classes

All Applications for our Autism Class will only be accepted after the 3rd November 2025 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 24th November 2025.
Any applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.


All applicants to the Autism Classes will be notified of an offer of a place or refusal of admission by 15th December 2025.

Successful applicants to the Junior Infants Group must confirm their acceptance of an offer of admission by completing and returning the Acceptance Form on or before 29th December 2025.

Applications to other classes

All applications for admissions to other classes (Senior Infants to 6th Class) will be accepted after 12th January 2026 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 2nd February 2026. Any applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

All applicants to these classes will be notified of an offer of a place or refusal of admission by 23rd February 2026.

Successful applicants to these classes must confirm their acceptance of an offer of admission by completing and returning the Acceptance Form on or before 9th March 2026.
Please be reminded that, in line with the school’s admission Policy and the Education (Admission to School) Act 2018, all waiting lists going forward are only in place for the academic year to which they apply. Therefore, if a child is seeking a place in the Autism Class and is not successful in a given academic year or is on a waiting list for this year, a new application must be made for the following year.
