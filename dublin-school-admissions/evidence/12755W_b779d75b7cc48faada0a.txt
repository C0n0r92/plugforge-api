           Procedure for All Admissions to Kildare Place School
How do I apply to the school?
  •   All admissions details and forms are on the school website, please consult the
      Admissions section of our website. This section contains comprehensive information
      including:
          o Annual Admissions Notice
          o Admissions Policy
          o Admissions Procedure
          o Application Form
          o Parish Boundary Map
          o 2km Boundary Map
  •   The school will have a set time each year in which it will be accepting applications for the
      upcoming September.
  •   Prior to the admissions date you are asked to simply download and print the application
      form. You are then asked to complete the form, submitting it in hard copy with all relevant
      supporting documentation.
  •   Please note only fully completed hard copy application forms with the correct supporting
      documentation will be accepted, and only during the dates specified in the Admissions
      notice.
  •   Applications will not be accepted by hand to the school office they may be posted to
      Kildare Place School, 96 Upper Rathmines Road, Dublin 6, D06 XE18, or dropped into
      the blue post box on the front gate of the school.
  •   You will be notified of your applications status in the timeframe outlined in the annual
      Admissions notice.
  •   Please note we would recommend that only copies of the important supporting
      documents are included, as no documents will be returned.
  •   The closing date will be strictly adhered to and late applications will not be accepted.
  •   For admissions queries for classes other than Junior Infants, please contact the school
      directly on [school email omitted] or complete the online form.
  •   Finally, we would ask you to read thoroughly all of the documentation prior to completing
      and submitting your application form.
