All applications to the Junior Infants in River Valley CNS will only be accepted after the 02/11/2020 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 11/01/2021. Applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.
All transfer applications to the Junior Infants and Senior Infants, in River Valley CNS will only be accepted after the 02/11/2020 and the closing date for receipt of applications is the 15/06/2021. Applications received after this date will be considered and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.

All applicants to the Junior Infants Class will be notified of an offer of a place or refusal of admission by:
25/01/2021

Successful applicants to the Junior Infants Group and/or Special Class must confirm their acceptance of an offer of admission to River Valley CNS by completing and returning the Acceptance Form on or before:
08/02/2021
Note: Failure to accept an offer of admission before the relevant deadline set out above may result in the offer being withdrawn.
