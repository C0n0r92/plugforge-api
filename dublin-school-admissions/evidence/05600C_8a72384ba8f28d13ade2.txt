Dublin PART 1 – Admissions to 2027/2028 school year Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2027/2028 The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 7th Oct 2026 The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 4th Nov 2026 In the event of the school being oversubscribed, the school will cease accepting applications after Nov 4th 2026. The school will continue to accept applications subject to the availability of places up to the end of June 2027. The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 18th Nov 2026 The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 4th Dec 2026 Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy. Special Class Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2027/2028 The following are the dates applicable for admission to the school’s Special Class which caters for children with Autism. The school will commence accepting applications for admission to the special class on 6th Jan. 2027 The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to the special class on 27th Jan. 2027 The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application for admission to the special class is 12th Feb. 2027 The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 26th Feb. 2027 *Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn Number of places being made available in 2027/2028 The number of places being made available in junior infants is 26 The number of places being made available in the special class catering for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder  is 1 Directions to our school Contact Convent Road
