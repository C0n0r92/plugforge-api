• Children must have reached the age of four before 1st June on the year of enrolment .
• Some local HSE criteria are in play for places in the Language class. Please see website
  for details
• Parents/guardians of children who live in the parish of Springfield must submit evidence of
  home address upon application, i.e. one current utility bills in the name(s) of the child's
  parents/guardians - no substitute documentation will be accepted. Offers of places will be
  based on this evidence and will be withdrawn if this information is subsequently found to
  be false.

• Applicants must indicate their acceptance of compliance with the schools Code of
  Behaviour and the Friendship & Inclusion (Anti-Bullying) Policy specifically and general
  acceptance of other school policies.
