     Criteria for enrolment in Autism Class in St. Francis Xavier Junior National School

 Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for
 enrolment to an autism class, incorporating the Department of Education and NCSE,
 policies and circulars, Circular Letter 0039-2025 EN final.pdf are as follows:

    1. Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-
       disciplinary report).

    2. A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of
       need/s evidenced in the professional reports.

    3. Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
       recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most
       appropriate to best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same.

    4. A letter of eligibility from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and
       that the child has the required diagnosis and recommendation for a special class
       for autism is required for special class or special school placement.

In the event that there are two or more students tied for a place or places in any of the
selection criteria categories above (the number of applicants exceeds the number of
remaining places), places will be offered or added to a waiting list in order of age, the eldest
being first.
