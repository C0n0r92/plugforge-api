                                  Annual Admissions Notice
The following is the Annual Admissions Notice for applications to Setanta School for the school year
September 2027 – June 2028.
Applications will be accepted from October 1st 2026 to February 2nd 2027.
An application will be valid when the documents listed below have been received by the school by the
final date for receipt of applications outlined in this notice.
Number of expected places available in Setanta School, September 2027: 15


Required Documentation
    1. Fully completed application form signed by one or both parents/guardians.
    2. A birth certificate for the student.
    3. Proof of address for the student (utility bill, social welfare letter or similar, dated within the
       last four months).
    4. Professional report(s) (no more than two years old) outlining:
            o   A diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder by a clinical psychologist or multi-disciplinary
                assessment.
            o   A demonstration of the complexity of the child’s overall level of need(s) evidenced in
                the professional reports.
            o   Where necessary, a clear professional recommendation for a special school
                placement to best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale.
    5. A letter from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and that the child has the
       required diagnosis and recommendation for a special school for Autism and complex needs.


The applicant shall be notified of the decision of their application by March 5th 2027.
The applicant must confirm in writing their intention to accept the offer of admission within 7 days
of the decision date.
Setanta School will cooperate with the NCSE in the performance by the Council of its functions under
the Education for Persons with Special Educational Needs Act 2004 in relation to the provision of
education to children with special educational needs, including in particular by the provision and
operation of a special class or classes when requested to do so by the Council. Setanta School will
comply with any direction served on the board or the patron under section 37A and 67(4)(b).


This Admissions Notice has been ratified by the Board of Management of Setanta School .
