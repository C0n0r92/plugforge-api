Pelletstown ETNS is participating in the Dublin D15 Autism Class Application Trial (ACAT).
Each school parents/carers apply to will process the application in accordance with the individual
school’s own admissions policy. The school (not the NCSE) will let parents/carers know the
outcome of their application.
The aim of the D15 ACAT is that parents/carers will only need to complete one application form for all
17 participating schools.

                  Criteria for enrolment in autism class in Pelletstown ETNS:

      Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for
  enrolment to an autism class, incorporating the Dept. of Education and NCSE. policies and
               circulars, Circular_Letter_0039-2025_EN_final.pdf are as follows:
     1. Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-
         disciplinary report)
     2. A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of need/s
         evidenced in the professional reports
     3. Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
         recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate to
         best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same.
     4. A letter of eligibility from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and that the
         child has the required diagnosis and recommendation for a special class for autism is
         required for special class or special school placement.


      Oversubscription priority criterion applied in Pelletstown ETNS Autism Class:
  As per the school’s Admissions Policy, the following priority criterion is applied in the event
  of there being more applicants for an available Autism class place.

  Priority Category 1: Applicant students enrolled in and attending mainstream classes in
  Pelletstown ETNS, who meet the criteria for enrolment for the Autism class

  Priority Category 2: Students who have been offered a place in a mainstream class for the coming
  year in Pelletstown ETNS, who meet the criteria for enrolment for the Autism class

  Priority Category 3: Applicant students who are siblings of children, enrolled in the school at the
  same time who meet the criteria for enrolment for the Autism class

  Priority Category 4: Applicant students living in the school planning area as defined by the
  Department of Education i.e. Pelletstown – to include the following areas; Phoenix Park
  Racecourse, Martin Savage Park, Rathborne and Royal Canal Park, who meet the criteria for
  enrolment for the Autism class

  Priority Category 5: All other applications received within the timeframe set out in the Annual
  Admission Notice, who meet the criteria for enrolment for the Autism class

  Oversubscription: In the event that there are two or more students tied for a place or places in any
  of the selection criteria categories above (the number of applicants exceeds the number of
  remaining places), places will be offered or added to a waiting list by the earlier date of birth of the
  student.

  Priority ranking will be given to the child who is no older than the oldest child enrolled in the
  Autism Class. If there are two or more children with the same date of birth, a lottery will be used to
  determine the order that these places will be offered or added to the waiting list.

  In the event that there are two or more students tied for a place or places in any of the selection
  criteria categories above (the number of applicants exceeds the number of remaining places),
  places will be offered or added to a waiting list by the earlier date of birth of the student.
