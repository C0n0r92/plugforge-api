    DANU COMMUNITY SPECIAL SCHOOL ANNUAL ADMISSIONS NOTICE FOR 2026/2027

The following Annual Admissions Notice has been prepared by the Board Of Management of Danu
Community Special School in respect of the admission of students for the school year 2026/2027.

                                   This notice was published on 24/09/2025.

                Where to find the school’s Admission Policy and Application Form
A copy of the school’s Admission Policy for the 2026/2027 school year is available to download at our
school website www.danucss.ie. The application form for admission to Danu Community Special School,
for 2026/27 is available at: www.ncse.ie and www.danucss.ie.

Danu Community Special School is participating in the Dublin D15 Autism Class Application Trial
(ACAT). Each school that parents/guardians apply to, will look at the application using its own admissions
policy. The school (not the NCSE) will let parents/guardians know the result of their application. The aim is
that parents/guardians will only need to complete one application form for all 18 participating schools. The
schools participating are:

                                              Late Applications
Applications made after the closing date of 31/10/2025, will be processed as late applications and will be
processed in accordance with the school’s admission policy.
Applications for admission received between 1/11/2025 and 13/04/2026 will be notified of the decision on
these applications by 30/04/2026. Applicants must confirm acceptance of any offer of admission issued within
7 days from the date on which the offer of admission has been made. Decisions on late applications will be
made in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy. Failure to accept an offer within the applicable
prescribed period above may result in the offer of admission being withdrawn.
