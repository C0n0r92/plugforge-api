Supporting Documentation
In order for an application to be considered complete, parents/guardians must submit:

   A completed Application Form.
   A copy of the applicant's Birth Certificate or Adoption Certificate.
   Proof of address where priority based on parish residence is being claimed.
   Any additional documentation requested by the school.

Applications which are incomplete may not be processed.

*Important Notice*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in
