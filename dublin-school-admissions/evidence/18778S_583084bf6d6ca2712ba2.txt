St. Mochta’s NS is participating in the Dublin D15 Autism Class Application Trial (ACAT).
Each school parents/guardians apply to will look at the application using its own admissions policy.
The school (not the NCSE) will let parents/guardians know the result of their application. The aim is
that parents/guardians will only need to complete one application form for all 18 participating schools.

         Criteria for enrolment in autism class in St. Mochta’s National School:
  Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for
    enrolment to an autism class, incorporating the Dept. of Education and NCSE.
     policies & circulars, Circular_Letter_0039-2025_EN_final.pdf are as follows:
    1. Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-
       disciplinary report)
    2. A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of
       need/s evidenced in the professional reports
    3. Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
       recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate
       to best meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same.
    4. A letter of eligibility from the NCSE confirming that the child is known to them and
       that the child has the required diagnosis and recommendation for a special class
       for autism is required for special class or special school placement.
