                       Admission Policy and Application Form

A copy of the school’s Admission Policy and the Application Form for Admission and
Developmental Checklist for the 2027/2028 school year is available as follows: –

To download at: www.stkiliansjns.ie

On request: By emailing [school email omitted] or writing to: The Secretary, St. Kilian’s
Junior School, Kingswood Heights, Dublin24, D.24H303.

You are required to submit all the following documents to the school within the time frame
indicated on the admissions notice.

Fully completed Application Form

Photocopy of child’s Birth Certificate

Proof of address, 2 different documents (only if residing within the parish boundary of
Kilnamanagh-Castleview) – see list of acceptable documents on application form checklist

Incomplete applications cannot be accepted. You must supply all accompanying
documentation.

Children who will reach the age of 4 by March 1st 2027 will be given priority in the first
instance, should demand exceed the number of places available, prior to the criteria
in section 6 being applied.

Our classes are deemed full when there are 28 children in the class.
