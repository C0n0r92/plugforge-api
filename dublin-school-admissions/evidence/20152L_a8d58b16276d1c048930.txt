Special Class Application and Decision Dates for Admission to the 2027/2028 School Year
The following are the dates applicable for admission to the school’s special class which caters for children with ASD. The Special Class in the North Dublin Muslim National School is designated for students who have been formally identified by the National Council for Special Education (NCSE) as requiring a placement in a special class attached to a mainstream school and therefore have a diagnosis of and a letter of eligibility. 

The school will commence accepting applications for admission to the special class on
01/10/2025
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to the special class on
31/12/2026
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application for admission to the special class is
29/01/2027
The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission to the special class is*
19/02/2027
*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn.
