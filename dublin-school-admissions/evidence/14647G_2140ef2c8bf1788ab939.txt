PART 3 - Application Requirements
All applications for a place in the school shall be submitted to the School Principal on the approved Application
Form - available on the school website.


Only those Application Forms that have been properly completed, signed, dated and accompanied by the
relevant supporting documentation will be considered.


For an application to be considered complete, all required accompanying documentation must be provided
before the closing date.
