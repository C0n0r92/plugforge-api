All applications to the Junior Infants or the ASD Special Classes in Scoil Chormaic CNS will only be accepted after

All transfer applications to Senior Infants, First Class, Second Class, Third Class, Fourth Class, Fifth Class or Sixth
Class in Scoil Chormaic CNS other than to the Special Classes, will only be accepted after the 13/12/2023 and the
closing date for receipt of applications is the 22/12/2023. Applications received after this date will be considered
and processed as late applications in accordance with the school’s Admission Policy.


Please be reminded that, in line with the school’s Admission Policy and the Education (Admission to Schools)
Act 2018, all waiting lists going forward are only in place for the academic year to which they apply. Therefore,
if a child is seeking a place in the ASD Special Classes and is not successful in a given academic year or is on a
waiting list for this year, a new application must be made for the following year.
