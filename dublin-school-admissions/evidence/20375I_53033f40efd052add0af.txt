Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2026/2027 school year

Applications must be submitted in hard copy to the address above and must include:
A completed Application Form for Admission
A recent documented and confirmed diagnosis of ASD (photocopy will suffice).
A written recommendation from a certified psychologist for an ASD-specific education in a special school. 
Letter of eligibility from the NCSE
Late Applications: In accordance with the school’s Admission Policy, the school will not consider late applications. Nor will the school accept incomplete applications that do not include the documents outlined above.

Data Protection: As per our data protection policy, all applications will be retained for the duration of the school year (i.e. until end of June 2027), at which point they will be destroyed.
*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn.
