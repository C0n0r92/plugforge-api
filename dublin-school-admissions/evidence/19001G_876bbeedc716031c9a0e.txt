Number of places being made available in 2027/28

The number of places being made available in Junior Infants is
30

Applications for places in classes from Senior Infants to 6th Class may be made at any time throughout the school year. Places, where available, will be offered in accordance with the Admissions Policy of Ballyboughal NS. 
Applications for places in our Special Class for Children with Autism will only be considered in accordance with the terms of our Admissions Policy. There are no places available in our existing Special Class for 2027/28. Ballyboughal NS has been requested to open a second Special Class for Children with Autism. We do not know at this point when we will be in a position to open the class. Applications may be submitted for the waiting list for our current class and for a possible new class which may be opened in 2027/28.
