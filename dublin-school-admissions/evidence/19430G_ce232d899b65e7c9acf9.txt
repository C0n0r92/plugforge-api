Number of places being made available in 2025 – 2026 school year

The number of places being made available in junior infants                26

 Children who are four years of age on or before 31st March in the year they start school
 will be accepted in Junior Infants. Exceptions may be made if there are available places
 in the class and where there are children mature enough to commence school.
Additional information regarding admissions:
The Board of Management will determine the number of children to be enrolled on a yearly
basis. The school will be guided by the following factors in this decision:

              •     the total number of children enrolled in the school
              •     the Departmental guidelines for appointing staff
              •     other relevant conditions such as the size of classrooms, the children’s
                    health and safety, special needs among the pupils in each class
                    available resources in the school


Places will be offered according to the following priorities:

              •     Children in the area who are being brought up through the medium of
                    Irish and siblings / stepsiblings of current pupils of the school
              •     Children of permanent staff in the school
              •     Children in the area whose families have a particular interest in the Irish
                    language
                       e.g: at least one parent is fluent in Irish
Where the number of applicants exceeds the number of places available, places will be
allocated according to the following criteria:

- the child’s age

- participation in relevant language/cultural activities e.g attending Irish classes
Applicants for Junior Infants must be due to be 4 years of age before the 31st March for the
year for which they are applying. Priority will be given in the following order:

    •   Children who have attained a level of fluency in the Irish language indicative of
        what would be expected of a student who uses the Irish language as a normal
        means of communication in a non-educational environment, taking into account
        the age and any special educational needs of the student concerned and that the
        said fluency would be likely to regress were the student not admitted to an Irish
        language school.
    •   A sibling / stepsibling of a pupil who is attending the school or of a pastpupil who
        attended the school for at least two years who will be 4 years of age before 31st
        March in the year for which they are applying for a place and the children resident
        in the Parishes of Our Lady of Victories and St. Pappin’s.
    •   Children of staff in the school
    •   Other applicants whose applications were received in the timeframe outlined in
        our Annual Statement who will be 4 years of age before 31st March in the year for
        which they are applying for a place.
