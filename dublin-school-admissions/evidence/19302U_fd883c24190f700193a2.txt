Admissions to the 2023/2024 school year Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2023/2024 The following are the dates applicable for admission for Early Start and Junior Infants. The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 17/01/2023 The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 28/03/2023 The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 11/04/2023 The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 25/04/2023 Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy. Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn. Number of places being made available in 2023/2024 Number of places being made available in The Early Start is 30 . There are 15 morning slots, from 08.50-11.20 a.m. There are 15 afternoon slots, from 12-2.30p.m. Number of spaces being made available in Junior Infants is 20. The following are the dates applicable for admission for the ASD class. The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 17/01/2023 The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 14/02/2023 The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 28/02/2023 The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 21/03/2023 Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy. Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn. Number of places being made available in 2023/2024 Number of places being made available in the ASD class is currently 0 as there are 6 pupils enrolled.
