                        PART 1 - Admissions to the 2027/2028 school year

 Application and Decision Dates for Admission to Mainstream classes for 2027/2028
 The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants:

   The school will commence accepting applications for admission on                           08/10/2026
   The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on                             29/01/2027
   The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is      12/01/2027
   The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is     19/02/2027


 *Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being
 withdrawn

 Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the
 school’s admission policy.

 Application and Decision Dates for Admission to the ASD Class 2027/2028
 The following are the dates applicable for admission to Naíscoil Íde’s special class which caters for
 children with ASD:

  The school will commence accepting applications for admission to the ASD class on               08/10/26
  The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to the ASD class on                 29/01/27
  The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is           12/01/27
  The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is          19/02/27

*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being
withdrawn
