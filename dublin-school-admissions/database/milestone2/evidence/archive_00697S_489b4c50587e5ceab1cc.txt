Applications for Autism Classes:
For 2024/2025 school year St. Brigid's NS will participate in the D15 Autism Class Application Trial.
13 primary schools, including St. Brigid's NS, and the NCSE are participating in the Dublin 15 Autism Class Application Trial.
The objective is that parents/guardians will only have one application to fill out for the 13 participating schools.
