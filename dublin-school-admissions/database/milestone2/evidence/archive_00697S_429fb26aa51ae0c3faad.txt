ACAT Admissions 2026/2027
Deputy
22-September-25
For 2026/2027 school year autism class admissions St. Brigid's NS will participate in the
D15 Autism Class Application Trial.
17 primary schools, and Danú special school are participating in the Dublin 15 Autism Class Application Trial. By
completing one ONLINE
application form and submitting it with relevant paperwork, you can apply to 18 schools.
This trial does not represent a common admissions policy. St. Brigid's NS maintains complete autonomy over our admission policy, criteria and oversubscription criteria. Please note that parental
participation in this trial, does not guarantee an autism class place for your child
.

St. Brigid's NS Admissions Policy 2025/2026:

Step 1:
All parents/guardians need to get ELIGIBILITY LETTER from NCSE for 2026/27 school year by the deadline of 1st of October 2025. This is done via PARENTS NOTIFY.
Watch a short video on how to complete the PARENTS NOTIFY process HERE.
ACCESS PARENTS NOTIFY ONLINE FORM HERE
(You will be redirected to NCSE website).
Step 2:
Complete the ONLINE APPLICATION FORM which will be live on our school website and
NCSE website
on 1/10/2025.
If you have difficulty completing this ONLINE form, you can email
D15ACAT@ncse.ie
and request assistance. You can come along to one of our Parent Support Sessions, which will be hosted by our SENOS. Email and posted applications will be accepted.
