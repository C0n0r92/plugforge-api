Broombridge ETNS is a developing school which opened in 2016 with one class of Junior
Infants. We are expanding on a yearly basis and when we reach capacity, will be a full two
stream school from Junior Infants to 6th Class with 2 special classes for Children with
Autism.
The school day starts at 8:30am for all classes and finishes at 13:10 for junior & senior infants
and at 14:10 for all other classes.
