Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2026/2027
The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants
1. The school will commence accepting applications for admission on
29
th

2025
2. The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on
17th October
2025
3. The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is
14th November
2025
4. The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is
14 days
Note: applications made after the 17th of October will be processed as late applications, the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy.
Failure of an applicant to accept an offer within seven days may result in the offer being withdrawn.
Special Class Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2026/2027
The following are the dates applicable for admission to the school’s Special Classes which cater for autistic children.

1. The school will commence accepting applications for admission to the special class on
1
st

2. The school shall cease accepting applications for admission to the special class on
30th April
2026
3. The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application for admission to the special class is
14th May
2026
4. The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is
7 days after the offer is made
Note: applications made after the 3th of May will be processed as late applications, the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy.
Failure of an applicant to accept an offer within seven days may result in the offer being withdrawn.
