In addition to the Admissions Notice, the following is also provided for your information


                           Criteria for enrolment in autism class in our school:

  Subject to sufficient places being made available in an autism class, the criteria for enrolment
      to an autism class, incorporating the Dept. of Education and NCSE policies & circulars,
   https://www.gov.ie/en/circular/f16ca-new-measures-to-support-the-forward-planning-for-
                            special-education-provision/ are as follows:

     1. Diagnosis of Autism: DSM IV/V or ICD 10/11 (psychologist, psychiatrist, multi-
        disciplinary report)
     2. A demonstration of the understanding of complexity of the child’s overall level of need/s
        evidenced in the professional reports
     3. Given the severity or complexity of the child’s support needs, a clear professional
        recommendation as to what educational placement type would be most appropriate to best
        meet the child’s needs, along with the rationale for same


 Admissions Criteria in our school
   1. Mary Mother of Hope JNS is a junior school and does not discriminate where it refuses to
       admit a child from 3rd – 6 class applying for admission to this school.
   2. Applicants must be at least 4 years of age before the first of June of the year in which they
       apply
   3. Applicants must not be more than 8 years of age at the start of the school year for which they
       are applying,
   4. No child will be retained in the special class beyond the 30th of June after their 9th birthday.


 Oversubscription priority criterion applied in our school
 To be applied in the event of there being more applicants for an available special class place. Listed in order of
 priority considerations
         1. Children already enrolled in mainstream classes in Mary Mother of Hope Junior National
            School, who have a diagnosis of ASD and a recommendation for a special class placement.

         2. Children with a diagnosis of ASD and a recommendation for a special class placement living
            within the catchment area and brothers & sisters (including step-siblings & foster children
            resident at same address) of children currently enrolled in Mary, Mother of Hope Schools
            with a diagnosis of ASD and a recommendation for a special class placement

         3. Children of current staff members of Mary, Mother of Hope schools (including step-siblings
            & foster children resident at same address) with a diagnosis of ASD and a recommendation
            for a special class placement

         4. All children from outside the catchment area with a diagnosis of ASD and a
            recommendation for a special class placement

    In the event of the number of children in category (1) exceeding the number of places available in a given year,
    the Board of Management will offer places based on chronological age of the children with the youngest
    applicants given a place first.
    The same criteria will be adopted in relation to category (2) and then (3) and then (4) until all places are filled.
    This process will be repeated each time a place becomes available in the ASD class.
    Where two children are tied for one place, the place will be allocated by lottery
    Please see Mary Mother of Hope JNS Admissions Policy for all details.
