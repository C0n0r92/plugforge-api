Planning for new ASD Special Class

The Board of Star of the Sea B.N.S. plans to open a partial ASD Special Class comprising three
pupils in temporary accommodation on 24th May 2021 in advance of necessary works to create
adequate facilities on site for a total of six pupils. The Board is working to expedite these
necessary works as quickly as possible and, subject to all necessary consents and approvals
from third parties and completion of the works, aims to be in a position to open the six pupil
class in September 2021.

Admission Procedures


Procedure (Junior Infant Mainstream Class):


   (a) Parents/Guardians must complete and submit an Admission Application Form for the
       year of entry (available on the school website) accompanied by a copy of the boy’s
       birth/adoption certificate which may be scanned (and emailed) or photocopied (and
       posted) to the school, and must obtain a letter of acknowledgment. In the event of any
       dispute, only a letter of acknowledgment will be taken as proof of a valid application
       having been made.


   (b) Completed Admission Application Forms and accompanying certificates must be
       received by the school before the closing date for applications, which is on or before
       3pm on the 25th of November 2020. Responsibility for ensuring that the school receives
       the requested documents by the closing date lies with the Parent/Guardian making the
       application.

   (c) Details of boys for whom completed Admission Application Forms and accompanying
       certificates have been received by the deadline will be placed on an applications list.
       Applications will not be recorded on the list until such time as all requested information
       has been received. The placement of any boy’s name on the list merely confirms receipt
       of a completed application. The applicant will then be assessed for eligibility on the
       basis of the criteria outlined in the admission policy.

       of the outcome of their application. Offers must be accepted within the time specified
       in the letter of offer. In the event of an offer not being accepted within the specified
       period, the right to a place is automatically forfeited.


Procedure (Special Class):
   (a) Parents/Guardians must complete and submit to the school an Admission Application
       Form for the year of entry (available on the school website) accompanied by a
       document showing proof of address, a copy of the boy’s birth/adoption certificate and
       the professional report(s) stating that a place in a special class within a mainstream
       school is both suitable and necessary for the child. Certificates and reports may be
       scanned (and emailed) or photocopied (and posted) to the school. Parents must obtain
       a letter of acknowledgment. In the event of any dispute, only a letter of
       acknowledgment will be taken as proof of a valid application having been made.


   (b) Completed Admission Application Forms and accompanying reports and certificates
       must be received by the school before the closing date for applications, which is on or
       before 3pm on 9th April 2021. Responsibility for ensuring that the school receives the
       requested documents by the closing date lies with the Parent/Guardian making the
       application.

   (c) Details of boys for whom completed Admission Application Forms and accompanying
       certificates and reports have been received by the deadline will be placed on an
       applications list. Applications will not be recorded on the list until such time as all
       requested information has been received. The placement of any boy’s name on the list
       merely confirms receipt of a completed application. The applicant will then be assessed
       for eligibility on the basis of the criteria outlined in the admission policy.

   (d) Parents/Guardians will be notified by 28th April 2021 of the outcome of their
       application. Offers must be accepted within the time specified in the letter of offer. In
       the event of an offer not being accepted within the specified period, the right to a place
       is automatically forfeited.
