JUNIOR INFANTS Admissions to the 2023/24 school year
The following are the dates applicable for admission to Junior Infants for September 2023

The school shall cease accepting applications for admission at 3pm on                         Monday 30th January 2023
The last date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is    Friday 17th February 2023
