Broombridge ETNS is a developing school which opened in 2016 with one class of Junior
Infants. We are expanding on a yearly basis and will be a full two stream school from Junior
Infants to 6th Class when we reach capacity.
The school day starts at 8:30am for all classes and finishes at 13:10 for junior & senior
infants and at 14:10 for all other classes.
