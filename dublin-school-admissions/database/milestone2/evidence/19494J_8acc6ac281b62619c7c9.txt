     The following are the dates applicable for admission to 3rd class AND for BGNS Autism class

                                                                                        3rd March 2026

The number of places being made available in BGNS Autism class*                         2

     The following are the dates applicable for admission to 4th, 5th or 6th class

                                                                                        21st April 2026

                                                                                        16th June 2026

is                                                                                      23rd June 2026

     *Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being
     withdrawn
