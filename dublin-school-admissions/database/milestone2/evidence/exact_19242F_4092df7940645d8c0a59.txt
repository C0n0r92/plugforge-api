Admissions to the 2022-2023 school year
The school will commence accepting applications for admission on
1st October, 2021
CLICK BELOW.
New 2022/23 Junior Infant Admission Form
New 2022/23 Senior Infant/1st Class Admission Form
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on
1st November 2021
.
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 22nd
November 2021, or within three weeks of date of receipt application in the case of late applications.
The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is
two weeks* i.e. 6th December 2021.
*Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn
Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy
