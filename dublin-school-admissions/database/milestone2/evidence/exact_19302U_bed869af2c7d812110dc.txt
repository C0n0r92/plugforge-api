Admissions to the 2023/2024 school year
Application and Decision Dates for admission to 2023/2024
The following are the dates applicable for admission for Early Start and Junior Infants.
The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 17/01/2023
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 16/03/2023
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is  24/03/2023
The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 07/04/2023
Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy.
Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn.
Number of places being made available in 2023/2024
Number of places being made available in The Early Start is 30.
There are 15 morning slots, from 08.50-11.20 a.m.
There are 15 afternoon slots, from 12-2.30p.m.
Number of spaces being made available in Junior Infants is 20.
The following are the dates applicable for admission for the ASD class.
The school will commence accepting applications for admission on 17/01/2023
The school shall cease accepting applications for admission on 14/02/2023
The date by which applicants will be notified of the decision on their application is 28/02/2023
The period within which applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission is 21/03/2023
Note: the school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school’s admission policy.
Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period above may result in the offer being withdrawn.
Number of places being made available in 2023/2024
Number of places being made available in the ASD class is currently 0 as there are 6 pupils enrolled.
Any applications for 2023/2024 will be held on a waiting list for the 2023/2024 school year in the event that a place becomes available in the ASD class.
Attendance
We love having your children in school and we hope they love being here, so please send them every day!! Our secretary Lorna along with our Home School Liaison teacher are here to help you with all things attendance related. Please use the Aladdin app or call us to notify if any absences. Thank you!
Our attendance winners for term one 2022. Congratulations and well done boys!!!!
If your child is unwell and cannot come to school have a look below for some helpful hints and fun activities you can do to ensure that nobody is bored!!
If they are able, you can ask your son's teacher for some work for home!
These following links and websites maybe helpful to you during this time. Please don’t get bogged down, pick and choose what your child may benefit from. Remember- play, talk and relaxation are helpful too, compliment yourselves as parents and what you are doing for your child.
1. Mental well-being:
(a)Childline 1800666666 (b) Spunout text 18000280 (c) www.pietahouse.ie
2.Education:
(a) bitesize.co.uk (b) scoilnet (worldbook link here too)  (d) studyclix.ie (e) Khanacademy.com (f) Scratchmit.ed (comic and animation)
3. Play and relaxation:
(a) Bike ride (b) Read together (books or make up stories, retell your child stories from the past, your own or their grandparents) (c) Ask your child to teach  you something they like to do (d) Cook /bake together
(e) Ask your child what yoga they know from school, to teach you a move or sequence, if not watch YouTube yoga for kids. (f) cosmickidsyoga (age 3+)
M-F: 9am - 3pm
