  Criteria for Enrolment to our Special Class
  Applicants must be at least four years of age before the First of June of the year in which they
  apply and no more than eight years of age at the start of the school year for which they are
  applying. No child will be retained in the special class or school beyond the 30th of June after their
  9th birthday. Compulsory attendance at school does not apply until the age of six years.
      • Each child must have a primary diagnosis of Autism/ Autistic Spectrum Disorder using the
          DSM V or ICD 10 as set out by a professional approved by the Department of Education &
          Skills and a Multi Disciplinary assessment report.
      • A recommendation that a special class placement is best for the child must be clearly
          stated in the child's reports

  The catchment area, for enrolment purposes consists of the following housing estates in the parish
  of Huntstown: Castaheany & Hansfield estates (off Littlepace Rd.), Deerhaven, Hazelbury, Hunters
  Run, Littlepace, Pheasant Run.
